# Money Plan — Starting Today

Honest framing first: nothing legitimate reliably pays out *tomorrow*. But some actions started **today** can produce cash within **days**, and the plan below is ordered by speed-to-cash. It follows the app's own Phase 0 rule: **stabilize income and kill expensive debt before trading** — your own strategy page says "No trading yet — cannot afford losses", and that rule exists because trading under financial pressure is how small accounts die.

## Today (fastest cash, this week)

1. **Sell what you don't use.** Facebook Marketplace / Gumtree / Cash Crusaders. Electronics, furniture, tools, old phones. This is the only item on this list that can genuinely pay out within 24–48 hours.
2. **Optimize the Airbnb listing** (your Phase 0 priority #1): refresh photos in daylight, rewrite the title/description, check pricing against 5 comparable listings tonight, enable Instant Book, open up any blocked dates. A better listing earns from its very next booking.
3. **Map all debt in one table**: balance, rate, minimum, penalty. Highest rate gets every spare rand (avalanche). A settled 22% store card is a guaranteed, tax-free 22% return — better than any trade you could make this year.
4. **Cancel zombie subscriptions** — check bank statements for recurring debits you forgot about. Takes 30 minutes, pays every month.

## This week (cash in 1–4 weeks)

5. **Freelance dev work** — you're a working software engineer; this is your highest hourly-rate asset:
   - Tonight: complete Upwork profile + 2–3 portfolio pieces (WealthPulse itself is a portfolio piece).
   - Apply to 5 small, well-defined jobs per day; first jobs land in 1–3 weeks; payouts ~1 week after delivery.
   - In parallel, start Toptal/Turing screening (higher rates, longer pipeline, USD income — a natural rand hedge).
6. **Open-source bounties** (Algora, IssueHunt) — pick TypeScript/React issues; payouts on merge.
7. **Tell your network you're available** for weekend/evening freelance work. Referral gigs skip the platform queue and fees entirely.

## This month (building the base)

8. **EasyEquities TFSA with R100** (Phase 1 trigger): STX40 + STXWDM. Tiny amount, but it starts the habit and the tax-free clock. Only after high-interest debt is under control.
9. **Route every windfall** (sale proceeds, first freelance invoice) through a simple rule: 70% debt, 20% emergency buffer, 10% TFSA.

## What NOT to do (the "dire situation" traps)

- ❌ No leveraged forex/CFD trading — under financial pressure these are wealth destroyers, and SA is full of brokers marketing them to people in exactly your situation.
- ❌ No crypto gambling, no "signal groups", no paid courses promising income.
- ❌ Don't borrow to invest. Ever.
- ❌ Don't neglect the rental for screen-watching — your plan literally has this as a rule.
