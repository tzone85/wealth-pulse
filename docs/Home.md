The app tracks six phases, from **Phase 0: Stop the Bleeding** through **Phase 5: Abundance**. The dashboard is the instrument panel; the phases (see `/strategy` in the app) are the flight plan.

Current phase: **Phase 0** — stabilize finances, optimize the rental business, map all debt. No trading with money owed to creditors.
